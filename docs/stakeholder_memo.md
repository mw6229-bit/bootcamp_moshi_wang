# Stakeholder Memo

## Stakeholders
The main stakeholders are investors, traders, and portfolio managers. They are exposed to equity market risk and may be affected by sudden increases in market volatility.

## Decision Context
The analysis will examine whether VIX spikes are associated with higher downside risk in S&P500 on the next trading day. If the relationship is strong, stakeholders can use this information when deciding whether to reduce equity exposure or strengthen risk management.

## Expected Output
The final output will include the analysis results and visualizations comparing the next day's S&P500 returns after VIX spikes with normal trading days. The results will help stakeholders to evaluate whether VIX spikes provide useful information for short-term risk decisions.
